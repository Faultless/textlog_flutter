Two changes asked for by F-Droid review, and nothing else. Per-ABI version codes now follow their scheme — code x 10 + 1 for armeabi-v7a, + 2 for arm64-v8a — instead of the offsets Flutter picks. And the Android build no longer writes a block of dependency metadata into the APK signature for the Play Store to read, which their scanner rejects and which nobody here wanted anyway.
